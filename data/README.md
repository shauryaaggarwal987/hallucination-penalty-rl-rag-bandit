# Data

`questions.jsonl` contains the frozen 50-question bank. `split_manifest.csv` and `.json` record the permanent 34/7/9 train/validation/test split with no overlap.

The three small project-authored sample documents are in `sample_docs/`. The externally collected Wikipedia-derived raw texts are not redistributed because the stored manifest does not retain complete per-article URL, revision, and attribution fields. `corpus_manifest.csv` records the frozen document identifiers and overlap-inclusive chunk statistics needed to inspect or reconstruct the corpus.
